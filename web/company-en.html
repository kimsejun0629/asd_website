Get to know Korean Air Aerospace

As a leader in the global aerospace industry, we will open the future through technology that leads market change and a spirit of relentless innovation.

Welcome to Korean Air Aerospace Business Division.

Korean Air Aerospace is an aerospace systems integrator. We built Korea’s first aircraft — the 500MD helicopter and the F-5 fighter Jegong-ho — produced the UH-60 medium-lift utility helicopter and carried out final assembly of the NARO launch vehicle. Today we perform depot maintenance and upgrades on military aircraft and co-develop and produce commercial aircraft with international partners.

Drawing on our R&D capabilities and global competitiveness, we have developed the division-level UAV and the medium-altitude UAV, and we contribute to Korea’s self-reliant defense through special-mission aircraft modifications. In the era of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, we are forging new paths for Korea’s aerospace industry in urban air mobility (UAM), air traffic management, unmanned systems, AI, space launch vehicles and stealth technology.

Every member of the Aerospace Business Division works with dedication and passion to create value for our customers. Building on the technology and know-how we have accumulated and a spirit of innovation for the future, we will fulfill our role and responsibility in advancing Korea’s aerospace industry. Thank you for your continued interest in and support for Korean Air Aerospace.

Korean Air Lines Co., Ltd.Lim Jin-kyu, Head of Aerospace Business Division
