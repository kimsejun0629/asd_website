Technical Roles · Aerospace

Our aerospace technical staff work across four functions — management, engineering, quality and R&D — covering commercial aircraft parts production, military aircraft maintenance, and the development of manned and unmanned aircraft and space launch vehicles.

Management

Management

Plans the division’s short- and long-term business, runs sales, manages program execution and builds detailed production plans for commercial aircraft parts and military aircraft maintenance. Oversees production and resources — man, machine, material and method — including progress, workforce and supply management.

Engineering

Engineering

Defines and directs technical support for commercial parts production and military aircraft maintenance, and writes and revises work instructions. Designs new processes, manages configuration changes and reviews and researches new technology development.

Quality

Quality

Reviews quality clauses, customer requirements and quality management system requirements to plan quality and inspection systems and prepare quality plans. Monitors production and maintenance results, investigates root causes of defects and puts corrective and preventive actions in place.

R&D

Research & Development

Researches and develops advanced manned and unmanned aircraft, space launch vehicles and satellites, and aircraft upgrades and modifications. Covers design, analysis, system integration and test & evaluation in aerodynamics, flight control, avionics and airframe structures.

Our training programs provide step-by-step job training along with individual and refresher courses, and employees build their capabilities through job rotation, overseas assignments and a range of development programs.

Korean Air’s HR system links development, evaluation and rewards into one integrated framework. Our technical workforce track is dedicated to developing specialists for maintenance and aerospace operations.

Evaluations center on competency and performance reviews, complemented by peer reviews and leadership surveys, to keep HR management fair and transparent.
